Our Story

Crafting Elixirs for the Modern Soul

Welcome to Secret Dose Cairo! Located in the vibrant heart of Heliopolis (85 Abu Bakr El Seddik St.), we are a premier specialty coffeehouse designed for connoisseurs of fine beans, immersive moody lighting, and sophisticated ambiance.

Whether you are looking for a sanctuary to focus on work, catching up with friends over pour-over masterpieces, or treating yourself to our signature cheesecakes and pizzas, every moment here is meticulously curated.

Live Jazz & Acoustic Sessions

Musical Nights at Secret Dose

Immerse your evenings in soul-stirring melodies. Every weekend, our Heliopolis sanctuary transforms into an intimate stage featuring exceptional live jazz players, saxophonists, and acoustic artists.

Sip on your favorite handcrafted pour-over or signature elixir while the warm amber lighting dances to the smooth rhythms of live jazz. Check our weekly schedule or drop by to experience Cairo's finest underground music vibe.
